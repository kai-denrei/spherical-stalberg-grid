# Plan — proximity mines (claymore, directional)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-04-mines-design.md`. Rulings are the
operator's (2026-09-04); nothing here re-decides them. Shelved and NOT to be
built: fused mines (A), two kinds (C), the chasing mine (D).

Standing rules that bind this work: arc LENGTH never a chord (`arc.js`);
pure decision module + thin effectful board; a fraction that is a fraction of
something variable stays a fraction; existing sounds only; `./scripts/bust.sh
--quiet` after any src/html/css edit and an ATOMIC commit of everything it
touched; `npm test` green.

## Task 1 — `src/mines.js` + `test/mines.mjs` (pure)

The whole rule set with no renderer. Field is `{ count, mines, next, queue }`;
a mine is `{ id, ci, pos, dir, laidAt, live, alive }`.

- `MINE_TUNE` exactly as §1 of the spec; `MINE_KNOBS` in the `knobs.js`
  idiom (strike.js is the model) so the feel tuner can reach it later.
- `makeField`, `layMine`, `armMines`, `inFan`, `inArc`, `trip`, `chain`,
  `stepChain`, `restock`.
- Geometry: the mine's front edge is `pos`; the fan is the wedge from `pos`
  along `dir` out to `fanDepth` cells, half-angle `arcDeg/2`; the blast is the
  same wedge out to `reach`. Both measured in ARC LENGTH via
  `projectToArc` — `s` in radians is arc length on a unit sphere, so
  `cells * cellSide` converts directly.
- Tests: every bullet of spec §6, plus a negative-`s` (behind the mine) case
  because `projectToArc` returns a signed `s` and taking `|s|` would make the
  claymore fire backwards.
- Register `test/mines.mjs` in `package.json`.

**Done when** `node test/mines.mjs` passes and `npm test` is green.

## Task 2 — the board: lay, arm, trip, blast

In `src/td-tab.js`:

- a `mineField` per run (reset where `ammo = 3` resets), `?mines=N` override;
- `layMineNow()`: the drop is one cell in front of the tank on the surface,
  facing `player.smoothDir` at that instant — derived from the render-side
  heading the same way `fire()` derives its aim. Refuse under the same gate
  the guns obey (`player.won || playerDown || paused || buildMode || deploy`).
  Refusals are silent except the HUD dim.
- key `N` (free) in `onKeyEvent`; `#td-pad-mine` beside FIRE/LASER via
  `root.querySelector(...).addEventListener('click', …)` (a tap, not a hold).
- per frame after enemies move: `armMines` (sound `tower_slow` on the ids that
  armed), then for each live mine test every alive hostile and the tank
  against `inFan`; first hit trips.
- on a trip: `damageEnemy(e, tNow, 1, true, 'mine')` per enemy target; a gate
  in the arc takes `popPods` + hp−1 through the SAME door a shell uses; the
  tank in the arc takes `playerHit('mine')`. `warnRing` at the mine +
  `makeDotBurst` at each target. `sfx.play('tank_main')`.
- the chain: ids returned by `trip` are queued and trip on FOLLOWING frames,
  front to back.
- HUD: the count beside `✦` ammo, dim at 0.

**Done when** `?mineprobe=1` logs a trip, its targets, a chain and the tank's
HP, headless.

## Task 3 — visuals

- the mine: a dot-cloud disc on the ground (the `makeDotBurst` idiom), dim
  ring while arming, lit when live;
- the fan: a red line fan on the ground from `pos` out to `reach`, built in
  cells along the arc so it follows the curvature; `params.mineArcs` +
  `?minearcs=0` off;
- the radar: friendly-blue arrowhead along `dir`, hollow while arming.
